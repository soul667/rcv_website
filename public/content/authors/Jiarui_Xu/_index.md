### About Me

I am currently a Research Assistant at the Robotics and Computer Vision (RCV) Lab at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). Prior to this, I was a master's student in the same lab and obtained my Master's degree in 2026. I previously worked on hardware acceleration for image processing.

My current research focuses on robotics, SLAM, and computer vision. And more specifically, I am currently exploring graph-theoretic and combinatorial optimization methods—such as maximum clique formulations—for robust 3D point cloud registration, along with algorithm acceleration across heterogeneous hardware platforms. In addition, I am actively applying these perception frameworks to real-world UAV-based 3D mapping and reconstruction systems.
