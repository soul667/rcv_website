### About Me

I am currently a master's student at the Robotics and Computer Vision (RCV) Lab at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). My research focuses on LiDAR-based 3D object detection and point cloud perception, particularly in scenarios where high-quality manual annotations are limited or unavailable. I am currently exploring unsupervised and self-training approaches for 3D object detection, including point-cloud-based pseudo-label generation, pseudo-label refinement, and trajectory-guided temporal consistency. My goal is to develop robust and efficient unsupervised perception methods that can improve the reliability of 3D object detection in autonomous driving and robotic systems.
